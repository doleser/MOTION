### Progress and issues

- **Use Case 3 (check-in).** The map, court screens, and check-in flow are connected to
  the backend (PRs #80 and #81, merged Nov 18).
- **E2E checklists.** We ran both checklists: 28 checks for Use Case 1 and 21 for Use
  Case 3. TODO(owen): results (how many passed, and the issues filed for failures).
- **Use Case 2 (ranked 1v1).** Challenges use real data now instead of sample data.
  - The challenges table and its functions are merged (PR #101). These cover sending,
    accepting, declining, and cancelling a challenge, the inbox, and the badge count.
  - The app calls them (PR #107), and the sample challenges are deleted.
  - A player can't be booked into two challenges at the same time (PR #109).
  - The match check-in screens use `match_readiness` to see whether the opponent has
    checked in (PRs #100, #104, #105, #108, #110).
- **Use Case 4 (results and Elo).** `report_result` creates the game (PR #101). Elo
  (PR #106 merged): `preview_elo`, `confirm_result`, and `dispute_result` are on `main`.
  A ranked confirm moves both ratings and writes both `elo_history` rows. A dispute
  voids the game and does not change Elo. 24-hour confirmation (PR #115 merged): a
  pending result reported at least 24 hours ago is confirmed. Ranked games move both
  ratings. Casual games, and a game missing a player, confirm with no Elo change. The
  sweep is not callable by clients. Dispute warnings (PR #116 merged): a dispute stamps
  `games.disputed_at` for both players and leaves Elo unchanged. A fifth dispute in the
  last month sets a 7-day ranked ban. A new ranked challenge is rejected during the ban.
  Casual challenges, and reporting or confirming an already-accepted ranked match, stay
  allowed. App and database CI on #116 passed. PRs #111 and #113 are still open: result
  reporting, confirm, and dispute screens, and the real Elo preview. Inbox polling
  (#112) is still open.
- **Performance requirement: met.** With 500 courts and 300 live check-ins, the slowest
  search in each batch of 20 took 14.43 ms (5 km, 10 courts) and 1.76 ms (50 km, 200
  courts). The requirement is under 1 s. A new database test fails CI if a search takes
  1 s or more (PR #102, open).
- **Database.** Hosted Supabase has all 20 migrations on `main`, including the Seattle
  court import (113 courts from OpenStreetMap and Seattle Parks). `main` has 260 pgTAP
  tests (up from 154), and CI passes.
  - Security: every `security definer` function now pins `search_path` to `''`, and a
    test enforces it (PR #103).
- **Peer review.** The README fixes are merged (PR #83), which closes #75–#77.
- **TA meeting (Nov 19).** We changed the spec to match the app: email and password
  sign-up with no verification, and 800 Elo when the assessment is skipped. We also
  agreed that no more user testing is needed, so the Week 7 user study and tester
  recruitment are cut.
- **Issues:**
  - Started implementation of KOTC feature (Daniel). King of the Court (#37) not
    started (Jace).
  - #38 (confirm_still_here pgTAP) is still open.
  - The challenge rules we built differ from Use Case 2 in the living doc in several
    places, listed in PR #107. Examples: there's no score target or Elo gap check, and a
    time conflict blocks the challenge instead of warning.
  - TODO(johnny): inbox polling (#112), real player profiles, #70, #58.
