### Progress and issues

- **Testing & CI (Oct 27 milestone).**

  - `check_ins`, with privacy rules: player counts are public, but only players checked in at

    courts is fixed; court data is still placeholder until the map connects to the database.

  - `check_in` and `check_out` are on `main` (PR #16). They reject readings more than 150 m
    from the court and courts that aren't active, close an expired check-in before a new
    one, and don't store coordinates.
  - The Supabase client for the app is in review (PR #14). Account creation endpoints are in
    progress (Joonho), and Johnny is connecting the screens to them.

      built.
  - **Elo and result confirmation is blocked** until the `games` and `elo_history` tables

  - **Jace and Joonho both planned the check-in service.** It's one Issue with both of them

  - **Task tracking moved to GitHub Issues this week.** Tasks lived in the group chat until

- **Set up GitHub Issues** for the Beta and Week 5 tasks, with owners and milestones.
- **Learned:**
  - A row-level security policy can't query its own table, itll just recurse forever.
    Moved that check into a small `security definer` function.
  - A partial unique index can't use `now()`, so an expired check-in still blocks the next
    one. `check_in` needs to close it first
  - Realtime change events follow RLS, so they can't keep public map counts live. For the
    Beta, the map uses re-fetch instead.
- **Issues:**
  - The living doc sync brought back errors I had fixed. They need fixing in the Google Doc
    itself.
